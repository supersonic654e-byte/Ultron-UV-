# Privacy Policy for Repository Content

This repository must contain only information approved for public disclosure.

## Publicly Acceptable

- General project problem and motivation
- High-level architecture
- Publicly available technology names
- Sanitized diagrams without private endpoints or names
- Synthetic or deliberately created demonstration data
- Test methodology and non-sensitive aggregate results
- Prototype photographs after background review and metadata removal

## Requires Anonymization or Permission

- Team-member names, emails, academic information, and contact details
- University, sponsor, partner, or company names and logos
- Photographs containing people, badges, documents, screens, room numbers, or identifiable facilities
- Market claims, health claims, performance claims, or cost claims that require evidence
- Internal project schedules and competition submissions
- Prototype photos that expose unpublished mechanical or electrical design details

## Must Remain Private

- Credentials and secret configuration
- VPN IPs, internal hostnames, access-control rules, and real endpoints
- Private hospital, client, or partner information
- Raw facility maps, rosbag recordings, telemetry, and operational logs
- Private datasets and model-training data
- Exact deployment-specific safety calibration values
- Proprietary firmware or business logic not approved for release
- Personal information without explicit consent
